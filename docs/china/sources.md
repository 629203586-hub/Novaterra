# Chongqing miniature — visual research and composition revision

Date: 2026-11-06. This is a Chongqing-inspired, compressed miniature, not a geographically faithful reconstruction. Liziba, Hongyadong and Raffles City are intentionally brought into one scene. All dimensions are visual estimates at miniature scale. No photos, external models, or runtime image downloads are used in the scene.

## Compared versions

The final direction restores the saved original China scene, rather than extending the rejected flattened rebuild. The original peninsula, solid layered hillside, 21 heritage building groups, 15 tower groups, red suspension bridge, waterfront, tiled roofs, station and camera perspective are retained. Source coordinates and the camera are uniformly scaled by 1.45, so the original visual proportions and composition remain unchanged. The saved original HTML has SHA-256 `fc00b1232f0546cd0c894c6e1ae0f8867eafc64c1b0869779f57282ac5ee7f77`.

Original geometry is submitted through the installed Little Worlds material batcher, with its three-step toon materials. The new runtime supplies camera interaction, motion preferences, recovery and disposal; 48 articulated Little Worlds characters replace the original static people. New Day / Sunset / Night lighting, soft shadows, restrained bloom and animated river reflections are retained. Original traffic and boat silhouettes are preserved.

Two local clearance repairs intentionally depart from the original: the Liziba train opening uses side walls instead of pillars crossing the guideway, and the eastern monorail bends around the Raffles-inspired towers. Piers are omitted inside occupied building footprints and extend to the authored terrain or underwater foundation. The original architecture is not relocated. Platform stopping is recalculated for the revised route. This is a compressed artistic composition, not a survey or engineering model.

## Photographs actually inspected

- [iChongqing: Hongyadong](https://www.ichongqing.info/attraction/hongyadong/). Inspected the full-size `stilted-buildings-daytime.jpg`, `stilted-buildings-sunshine.jpg`, and `stilted-buildings-day.jpg` photographs from its gallery. Observed stepped construction against a cliff; continuous timber galleries and lattice screens; plaster infill; closely spaced tiled roofs; lanterns; lower riverside traffic; planted cliff shoulders. Comparison criteria: stacked terraces, timber galleries, tiled roofs and a continuous riverfront. The user ultimately selected the saved original architecture and layout. The warm lower roofs deliberately retain the user's preferred first-version palette; real photographs also show charcoal-gray tiles.
- Additional official night and riverfront photographs inspected on 2026-11-06: [night facade](https://source.ichongqing.info/2019/10/stilted-buildings.jpg) and [layered riverfront](https://source.ichongqing.info/2019/10/stilted-buildings-views.jpg). Observed golden eave outlines, red lanterns and stacked galleries; used to judge the warm-night lighting while preserving the original geometry.
- [Travel China: Hongyadong](https://www.travelchina.org.cn/en/article/vF1cJZaYCtX8). Official tourism source confirming the stacked cliffside and top-to-bottom circulation. Used as structural context, not as measured architectural drawings.
- [Original Liziba photo essay](https://woshixiao.hateblo.jp/entry/2025/05/16/232943). Inspected the original photo [20250510175930.jpg](https://cdn-ak.f.st-hatena.com/images/fotolife/W/WoshiXiao/20250510/20250510175930.jpg). Observed pale residential upper floors, tan podium, red trim, green-and-white monorail, an actual open train passage and nearby green slope. The restored original uses its two-car train and station silhouette; only the open guideway passage and route clearance are repaired. Train and station details are simplified, not a precise rolling-stock replica.
- [Raffles City photograph and architecture article](https://www.metalocus.es/en/news/safdie-architects-raffles-city-chongqing-opens-worlds-longest-sidescraper). Photography dates are unknown except where the source identifies one. Ground-level photos are not interactive Street View. Unseen facades, engineering members, indoor layouts and exact terrain profiles are authored inferences. A wider first-version-style river palette is an artistic choice, not a claim that the actual Jialing River is always turquoise.

## Reused framework

The installed `little-worlds-3d` starter supplies the vendored Three.js 0.180.0 dependency, three-step toon material system, geometry batching, character factory and camera control. Original route and vehicle animation is adapted to the new runtime clocks. Chongqing architecture, terrain, routes, boats, monorail and lighting are authored adapters. The procedural soft-water implementation was adapted from the installed `visual-polish` asset, including a clipped island footprint and reduced reflection strength. The offline wrapper invokes the bundled Little Worlds HTML exporter; it does not rebuild a separate export graph implementation.
